Energy Model & Assumptions

This project uses a simplified mathematical model to estimate energy consumption during drone route planning.

  • Energy per grid move: 0.8 Wh/move
  • Assumed battery capacity: 100 Wh
  • Battery reserve: 20%
  • Usable battery energy: 80 Wh

Calculation Formulas

Estimated Energy (Wh) = Total Route Distance × 0.8

Battery Used (%) = (Estimated Energy ÷ 100) × 100

Battery Remaining (%) = 100 − Battery Used (%)

Energy Feasibility: The route is considered feasible under this model when estimated energy consumption is less than or equal to 80 Wh.

Important limitation: These values are simulation assumptions for an academic project, not measured specifications of a real drone. Actual energy consumption can vary with payload weight, flight speed, wind, hovering time, battery condition, and other operating conditions.
